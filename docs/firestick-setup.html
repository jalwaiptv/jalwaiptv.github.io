Fire TV & Firestick Setup

Connect the Fire TV device to a stable network and keep Fire OS updated. Use only applications you trust and that are appropriate for your subscription. Enter account or playlist information only into the intended application. If playback is unstable, restart the device and router and test the connection before changing application settings.
