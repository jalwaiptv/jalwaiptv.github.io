Buffering & Playback Troubleshooting

Start by testing your internet connection and restarting both the streaming device and router. Close unnecessary applications, prefer a stable wired or strong Wi-Fi connection, and check whether other devices are consuming significant bandwidth. If the problem persists across devices and networks, contact support with your device model and a description of the issue.
